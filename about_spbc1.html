About Second Providence

A worshipping, witnessing and welcoming church located in North Augusta, SC.

Welcome to Second Providence Baptist Church website!  We hope that your experience on the website will provide you with a sample of our Christian fellowship that you will be eager to visit with us in the flesh. The Second Providence Baptist Church is a worshipping, witnessing and welcoming church located in North Augusta, SC.

Our mission is to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and to advance the kingdom of God.   In doing so we are focusing on the body of Christ through spiritual fulfillment from a powerful pastor, teaching, global mission and interaction among the members and the community.
